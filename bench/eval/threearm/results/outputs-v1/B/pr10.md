Silence pacmd help stderr in pacmd and pactl completions

Redirect stderr to `/dev/null` for `pacmd help` in `share/completions/pacmd.fish` and `share/completions/pactl.fish`. When PulseAudio is not running, `pacmd help` prints `"No PulseAudio daemon running, or not running as session daemon."` to stderr into the user terminal.

Not tested: Tab completion for `pacmd` and `pactl` when PulseAudio daemon is not running.
