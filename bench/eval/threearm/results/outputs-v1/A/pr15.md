Truncate actual and expected diff strings at 2048 characters

Truncate `err.actual` and `err.expected` strings at 2048 characters in the base reporter before passing them to `jsdiff` (closes #3675). Synchronous diff calculation on huge strings previously blocked Mocha for several minutes.

- Reporters: truncate strings exceeding 2048 characters to `diffSize` and append `' ... Lines skipped'` in `lib/reporters/base.js`
- Tests: verify truncation on large `actual` and `expected` values in `test/reporters/base.spec.js`

Not tested: `npm test test/reporters/base.spec.js`.
