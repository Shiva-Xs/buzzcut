Silence stderr from pacmd help in completions

Redirect stderr to `/dev/null` for `pacmd help` in `share/completions/pacmd.fish` and `share/completions/pactl.fish`. When no daemon is running, `pacmd help` prints "No PulseAudio daemon running, or not running as session daemon." to stderr, leaking into the terminal during completion.

Not tested: tab completion for `pacmd` and `pactl` without a running PulseAudio daemon.
