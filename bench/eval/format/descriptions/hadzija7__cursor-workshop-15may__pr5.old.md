Let the cafe planner order a meal more than once

Each meal could only be added once, at quantity 1, and the spec listed repeat orders as the workshop extension; this adds them, keeping one row per meal. After "Add to plan", a card shows an −/quantity/+ stepper and an "In plan" badge instead of the disabled "Added" button, the live plan panel gets the same stepper plus a Remove button and servings per batch and per dish, and − at quantity 1 removes the row. `CafePlanner` holds `incrementLineQuantity`, `decrementLineQuantity` and `removeLine`, totals still come from `summarizePlan(planLines)`, and the spec now describes this as shipped behavior. Tried in a screen recording of add, +/−, a second meal and the panel controls ([demo](https://cursor.com/agents/bc-7ce603fb-a12d-4729-86c6-ef56f293c2ec/artifacts?path=%2Fopt%2Fcursor%2Fartifacts%2Fcafe-planner-multi-qty-demo.mp4)).
